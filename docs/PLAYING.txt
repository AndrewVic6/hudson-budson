HUDSON BUDSON: LOCKER RAID — FIRST PERSON
A separate first-person edition of the Locker Raid prototype.

START PLAYING
1. Unzip this folder.
2. In Godot, choose Import and select HudsonBudson/project.godot.
3. Open the project and press F5 (Run Project).
4. Move the mouse to look around. WASD moves relative to your view.

Tested with Godot 4.4.1, Compatibility renderer. This is an editable Godot
project, not a standalone executable. The hallway is built when you press
Play, so an empty editor viewport is normal. No plugins or paid assets needed.
Use a computer with a keyboard and mouse. Touch/controller controls are not
included. The previous third-person edition is still available separately.

YOUR FIRST MINUTE
Look left toward locker 101 near where you start. Walk close enough for the
crosshair to turn gold and show [C] OPEN LOCKER 101. Press C to open it.
The baseball bat equips automatically. Aim at a nearby zombie and left click.
Look in other numbered lockers for snacks, protection and other equipment.
Find the hall pass, then reach the exit pad at the far end of the school.
You can fight, distract or dodge zombies. Clearing all five is optional.

CONTROLS
Mouse                Look around
W / S                Walk forward / backward relative to your view
A / D                Strafe left / right without changing your aim
Shift                Sprint
Space                Jump
Left click / F       Swing your equipped weapon
Right click / Q      Hook a zombie near the crosshair; again to release
E                    Hold to reel in a hooked zombie
C                    Open the numbered locker under the crosshair
H                    Eat a stored snack if your health is below 100
G                    Throw a tennis ball in the direction you face
1 / 2 / 3            Equip tray / baseball bat / tennis racket
T                    Embarrass Hudson to fill the mortification meter
Escape / Tab         Pause, release the cursor, and open the tuning menu
R                    Restart with fresh inventory and default settings

Aim at a zombie's body. Weapons have different ranges; the bat reaches
farther than the tray. Attacks and fishing hooks cannot pass through walls.
The little crosshair flashes when a hit lands. A red edge flash means you
were hit. Food, armor and speed upgrades work just as in Locker Raid.

COMFORT AND PAUSING
Escape or Tab opens the pause menu. Adjust mouse sensitivity or field of view.
You can turn off the gentle weapon motion. The camera has no added head bob
or forced shake. Lower Zombie speed if you want more time to explore.
Click RESUME GAME or press Escape/Tab to return and capture the mouse.
Switching away from the game automatically pauses it. Win and defeat screens
release the cursor too. All settings and progress reset when you press R.

WHAT'S IN THE LOCKERS
Apple             Up to 15 health
Juice box         Up to 20 health
Lunch sandwich    Up to 35 health
Baseball bat      40 damage; longer reach and stronger knockback
Tennis racket     20 damage; fast swing and the strongest knockback
Tennis balls      3 throws; each distracts nearby zombies for 7 seconds
Padded backpack   30 protection; stacks up to 60
Fresh sneakers    20% faster movement
Notebook          25 mortification; a full meter gives a speed boost
Hall pass         Permission to escape through the exit

Each numbered locker provides loot once per run. New weapons equip when
found. You can switch among weapons you own. H chooses a suitable stored
snack, and never wastes a snack when health is full. The tray starts unlocked
and deals 25 damage. Locker contents are fixed to make learning repeatable.

EDITING THE GAME
scripts/player.gd     First-person camera, mouse look, movement and weapon view
scripts/main.gd       Hallway, aiming, loot actions, UI and pause settings
scripts/locker.gd     Lockers, opening animations and item reveals
scripts/inventory.gd  Item effects and weapon stats
scripts/crosshair.gd  Reticle and hit/damage feedback
scripts/art.gd        Editable character and item meshes
scripts/zombie.gd     Zombie movement, distractions and knockback
scripts/settings.gd   Starting values

For a first experiment, change the bat damage in inventory.gd from 40 to 30.
Press F5, try it, then decide which version feels better.

RECOVERY
Checkpoints/First-Person-Clean-Project.zip contains a clean copy of this
project. Unzip it into a separate folder and import its project.godot.

VERIFICATION
The included smoke_test.gd checks camera movement, mouse capture, aiming,
locker interaction, combat, inventory, pause, winning, losing and restart.
Run from a terminal with a graphical display (not --headless):
  godot --path HudsonBudson --script res://tests/smoke_test.gd
See Test-Results.txt for this build's results.

This remains a short single-player prototype with one hallway, per-run
inventory and no saved progression. Preview images are actual game renders
with staged positions and equipment for inspection.
